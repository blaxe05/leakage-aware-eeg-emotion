# Generated Figure Manifest

Figures in this directory are generated by
`../scripts/generate_nature_style_figures.py` unless noted otherwise.

## Data Sources

- `../04_extraction/extraction_master.csv`: 404 active included studies, 672
  active result rows, 98 meta-eligible studies, and 198 meta rows.
- `../06_quality/risk_of_bias_scoring.csv`.
- `../07_meta_analysis/meta_results.csv`.
- `../07_meta_analysis/moderator_analysis.csv`.

## Output Notes

- Figures are exported as PDF, PNG, and SVG where supported.
- Dataset counts are non-additive because one paper may report more than one
  dataset.
- The target-access and evidence-ladder figures encode protocol definitions;
  they are not performance rankings.
- The SEED forest figure shows individual study results and protocol-specific
  random-effects estimates.

See `../RUN.md` for regeneration commands and
`../09_manuscript/manuscript_data_traceability.md` for the source of each
manuscript figure.
